# Andrew Lam

**From operating records to clearer decisions.**

I'm an operations and supply-chain professional in Singapore, bringing **20+ years in logistics and operations** to practical analytics and applied AI. My experience spans cold-chain warehousing, grocery distribution, container operations and international freight forwarding.

I use **Python, SQL, Power BI and Excel** to investigate operating questions and build tools people can inspect. My next chapter connects operations intelligence, cloud engineering and the development of **VELYQUA** and **ORQEVIA**.
