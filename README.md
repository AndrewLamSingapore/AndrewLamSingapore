## Flagship project: VELYQUA

**Aquatic monitoring and decision support, with an emerging applied AI research direction.**

The current software prototype supports aquarium records, manual water tests, observations and care history. Its public walkthrough uses synthetic educational examples. AI-assisted monitoring, sensor fusion and intervention-outcome analysis are development and validation goals.

My next priorities are reliable data ingestion, traceable recommendations, cloud monitoring, human intervention records and follow-up outcome measurement. I aim to identify a specific customer problem, run a focused pilot and explore a paid pilot once the evidence supports it. Expansion into other industrial applications will depend on customer demand and separate technical validation.
